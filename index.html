Notes from
the wild.

Small observations about the strange, beautiful and fascinating world of plants.

01 — MOVEMENT
🌱

Plants can move.

The sensitive plant, Mimosa pudica, folds its leaves when touched. It can also react to vibrations.

A LITTLE BOTANICAL FACT
02 — COMMUNICATION
🍃

Invisible messages.

Some plants release chemical signals into the air when attacked by insects. These signals can affect nearby organisms.

A LITTLE BOTANICAL FACT
03 — SURVIVAL
🌵

Desert survivors.

Cacti have adaptations such as thick water-storing stems and spines that help reduce water loss.
